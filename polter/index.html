Privacy Policy

Polter File Automation
Last Updated: January 2025

Overview

Polter File Automation ("Polter", "we", "our", or "the app") is a desktop application that helps you automate file organization on your computer. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

Local Data (Stored on Your Computer)

  • File paths and names: The app monitors folders you specify and processes files according to your rules. File paths and names are stored locally in a SQLite database.
  • Activity logs: Records of file operations performed by the app are stored locally.
  • Application settings: Your preferences and rule configurations are stored locally.
  • AI provider API keys: If you use third-party AI providers, your API keys are stored locally on your device.

Data Sent to Third Parties

AI Services (Optional): If you enable AI-powered rule generation, natural language prompts and file paths (not file contents) may be sent to your chosen AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or local Ollama).

Error Reporting (Optional): If error reporting is enabled, application errors and system information may be collected. No file contents or personal files are included.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • File contents: We never read, analyze, or transmit the contents of your files
  • Personal information: We do not collect names, email addresses, or other PII
  • Usage analytics: We do not track feature usage or behavior patterns
  • Location data: We do not collect location information

Data Storage and Security

All application data is stored locally on your computer. Database files are stored in your user data directory. We recommend keeping your configuration files secure.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your Rights and Choices

All data is stored locally - delete the app and its data folder to remove all data. AI features and error reporting can be disabled.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us at support@localghost.store.
