Privacy Policy

Polter File Automation — Last Updated: June 2026

Overview

Polter File Automation ("Polter", "we", "our", or "the app") is a desktop application that helps you automate file organization on your computer. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information, both within the desktop app and on our marketing website.

Information We Collect

Local Data (Stored on Your Computer)

  • File paths and names: The app monitors folders you specify and processes files according to your rules. File paths and names are stored locally in a SQLite database.
  • Activity logs: Records of file operations performed by the app are stored locally.
  • Application settings: Your preferences and rule configurations are stored locally.
  • AI provider API keys: If you use third-party AI providers, your API keys are stored locally on your device.

Data Sent to Third Parties

AI Services (Optional): If you enable AI-powered rule generation, natural language prompts and file paths (not file contents) may be sent to your chosen AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or local Ollama).

Error Reporting (Optional): If error reporting is enabled, application errors and system information may be collected. No file contents or personal files are included.

Data the App Does NOT Collect

  • File contents: The app never reads, analyzes, or transmits the contents of your files
  • Personal information: The app does not collect names, email addresses, or other PII
  • In-app usage analytics: The desktop app does not track feature usage or behavior patterns
  • Location data: The app does not collect location information

Website Analytics

This section applies to our marketing website (the Polter landing pages on thelocalghost.com), not to the desktop app. The website uses Google Analytics 4 (Google's gtag.js, measurement ID G-38ZVSY9P7H) to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics may set cookies and collect standard web data such as your IP address, browser and device type, referring page, and the pages you view. This data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. We use it only in aggregate to improve the site and do not use it to personally identify you. The desktop application itself contains no website tracking or analytics.

If you contact us through our contact page or by email, we receive the information you choose to provide (such as your name, email address, and message) solely to respond to your inquiry. Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy (our payment processor and Merchant of Record); we do not store your full payment card details.

Data Storage and Security

All application data is stored locally on your computer. Database files are stored in your user data directory. We recommend keeping your configuration files secure.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your Rights and Choices

All data is stored locally — delete the app and its data folder to remove all data. AI features and error reporting can be disabled.
