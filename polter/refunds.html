30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to be completely satisfied with Polter. If you're not happy with your purchase for any reason, we offer a full refund within 30 days of your purchase date.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us at support@localghost.store with:

  • Your order number or the email address used for purchase
  • The reason for your refund request (optional, but helps us improve)

Refund Processing

  • Refunds are typically processed within 5–10 business days
  • The refund will be credited to the original payment method
  • Upon refund, your license key will be deactivated

What Happens After a Refund

After receiving a refund:

  • Your license key will be deactivated
  • You will no longer receive updates or support
  • You should uninstall the software from your devices

Exceptions

Refunds may not be available for:

  • Purchases made more than 30 days ago
  • Licenses obtained through promotional giveaways
  • Cases of license abuse or fraud
